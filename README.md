# MyWallet

A simple yet powerful mobile application for tracking your personal finances, managing expenses, and achieving your financial goals — all in one place.

---

## 📱 Overview

**MyWallet** helps users take control of their finances by providing an intuitive interface to monitor spending, manage savings, and plan budgets. Built with **Flutter**, it offers seamless performance in Android devices.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🧾 **Expense Tracking** — Log your daily transactions and categorize your expenses.  
- 💸 **Budget Management** — Set monthly budgets and get alerts when you’re close to exceeding limits.  
- 📊 **Analytics Dashboard** — Visualize spending patterns through clean charts and summaries.  
- 🎯 **Goal Setting** — Create and track savings goals for future purchases or milestones.  
- 🔔 **Smart Notifications** — Get reminders for due bills or upcoming expenses.  
- ☁️ **Secure Backup** — Sync and back up your data safely (optional cloud integration).  
- 🌓 **Dark Mode** — Switch between light and dark themes for better visibility.
